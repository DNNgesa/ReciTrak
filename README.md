<h3>ReciTrak is a restaurant production and supply-chain management platform that uses recipes and sales data to estimate resource use in a culinary establishment.</h3>

<h3>The platform uses recipes as the "truth" originator for the following:</h3>

<p>1 - Determine Cost of Goods</p>
<p>2 - Save recipe variations that can be recalled for different production scenarios</p>
<p>3 - Track inventory usage when compared with sales data</p>
<p>4 - Alert relevant stakeholders in cases of resource depletion to minimum levels and automatically generate orders for approval</p>
<p>5 - Evaluate menu item profitability by taking into account sales volumes, selling price, Cost of Goods and processing time</p>
<p>6 - Updates cost of resources by allowing direct linkage with supply-chain partners</p>
<p>7 - Offers exclusive connection to a vast supply-chain network for competitive sourcing</p>
<p>8 - Ensures consistency in production standards and accountability by acting as a training tool for new hires</p>
