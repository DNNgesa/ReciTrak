ReciTrak is a restaurant production and supply-chain management platform that uses recipes and sales data to estimate resource use in a culinary establishment.

The platform uses recipes as the "truth" originator for the following:

1 - Determine Cost of Goods
2 - Save recipe variations that can be recalled for different production scenarios
3 - Track inventory usage when compared with sales data
4 - Alert relevant stakeholders in cases of resource depletion to minimum levels and automatically generate orders for approval
5 - Evaluate menu item profitability by taking into account sales volumes, selling price, Cost of Goods and processing time
6 - Updates cost of resources by allowing direct linkage with supply-chain partners
7 - Offers exclusive connection to a vast supply-chain network for competitive sourcing
8 - Ensures consistency in production standards and accountability by acting as a training tool for new hires
